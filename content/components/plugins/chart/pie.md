## Simple

A pie reads the first numeric column: one slice per row. Hovering a slice lifts it.

::preview{example="echart/pie-simple" inline}

## Donut, legend on the side

`type="donut"` opens the center. Here `options` moves the legend to the right and the donut to the left.

::preview{example="echart/pie-donut" inline}
