## Stacked and horizontal

`stacked` piles the series of each category; `horizontal` lays the bars down, which suits long category names.

::preview{example="echart/bar-stacked" inline}

## On a time axis, with zoom

`datetime` turns the index into a time axis; `zoom` adds the slider. Six months of daily bars stay readable.

::preview{example="echart/bar-zoom" inline}
